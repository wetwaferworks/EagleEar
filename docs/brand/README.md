# Eagle Ear brand assets (v7, natural badge)
Eagle Ear, the speech-to-text tool by **Wet Wafer Works**. Logo art by **CloudAtlas** for Wet Wafer Works. It's an original vector drawing with no stock pixels and no traced stock outlines.

Palette: eagle head #F7F5EF, feathers #D9D4C7, body #4A2E1A / #2B1A0F, beak #F5B921 / #D9940E, eye #F2B705, flag red #B22234, white #FFFFFF, navy #3C3B6E, rings orange #FF8A00 and navy #1E2A78, background Void #090724.

## Layered master (`eagle-ear-v7-layered.svg`, `-layered-dark.svg`)
These are Inkscape/Illustrator layers (`inkscape:groupmode="layer"`), bottom to top: background, flag, eagle body, head, feathers, beak/eye (brow, eye, beak, plus the white cheek-feather overlap at the beak base), rings. The layered SVGs have no text. Clip ids: c7 (badge circle), hc (head, used to clip the feathers).

## ICO files
`favicon.ico` and `eagle-ear-windows.ico` each contain 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px frames. Every frame is rendered natively at its size, with no downscaling. The 16-32 px frames come from `eagle-ear-v7-small-master.svg`, a closer crop with a bolder outline and thicker rings. 256 px is the largest frame the ICO format supports. Individual frames are in `icons/ico-frames/`.

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
- Orbitron ExtraBold for the wordmark (The League of Moveable Type)
- Saira SemiCondensed Medium for the tagline (Omnibus-Type)

Lockup SVGs keep live text. The PNGs were rendered with resvg.
